# empty-body.acpanim.gz

Text-art frames (120×34 characters, 12 fps, 120 frames) made from the first 10
seconds of the video **"Debating Is Man the Measure of All Things?"** by the
YouTube channel **IGG | Qizilbash** (<https://youtu.be/iK-_kgVuv6Y>). The speaker
is the executive chair of the ACP; the footage is **used with permission of the
ACP** in ACP apps such as this reader. These frames are not covered by the
program's GPL licence; the video itself is not included.

Regenerate with:

    tools/acp-anim https://youtu.be/iK-_kgVuv6Y --seconds 10 --fps 12 --cols 120 --rows 34 \
        --out assets/empty-body.acpanim.gz
