# 🔴 You found the wallpapers.

Not many people dig this far into `assets/`. Since you did, here's a little loot: backgrounds for a desktop, a phone,
or a tablet. The poster ones are cut from the same stock as the ACP Red Star roar-demo title card. Sunburst, gold
pinstripes, halftone grit, and a star that glows like it means it.

| variant | vibe |
|---|---|
| `title-shot/` | The title card itself: the logo, **ACP RED STAR**, and the slogan. |
| `logo/` | Just the star on the poster. Quiet, but it knows what it is. |
| `cosmos/` | Resonance meets the cosmos: a red nebula, a starfield, and one star burning brighter than the rest. |
| `cosmos-centered/` | Same nebula, with the star dead centre. |
| `nebula/` | A painted nebula in pink, violet, and peach. |
| `nebula-centered/` | The same painted nebula, heart of it dead centre. |
| `eclipse/` | A painted eclipse: rays, a cratered moon, and planets. |
| `eclipse-star/` | That eclipse, with the Red Star logo dimmed and worn where the sun was. |
| `wordmark/` | Almost black. Just the name, glowing softly. For people who like their desktop moody. |

Every variant comes in **5160×2160** and **3440×1440** (ultrawide 21:9) and **2560×1440** and **1920×1080** (16:9).
Each size was laid out on its own, not just cropped, and the outer edges are left clear for your desktop icons.
Phones and tablets have their own sheets too: **1290×2796**, **1440×3200**, **2048×2732**, and **2732×2048**, laid out for those screens rather than cropped from the desktop.
Peek at `contact-sheet.jpg` for the desktop set before you pick.

The poster variants are made with code, not painted: a little numpy, some Gaussian blur, and a lot of red. Nebula and eclipse are painted.

These come in **3440×1440** (ultrawide 21:9) and **2560×1440** and **1920×1080** (16:9) only. There is no **5160×2160**.
Peek at `poster-contact-sheet.jpg`.
